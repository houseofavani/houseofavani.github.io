The Designer

Avani Agrawal

I am a jewellery designer drawn to the world of luxury, craftsmanship, and timeless artistry. My work is rooted in creating pieces that embody elegance, refinement, and individuality, where every detail is considered with intention.

Inspired by heritage techniques, natural forms, and contemporary high jewellery aesthetics, I explore the harmony between structure, texture, gemstones, and intricate detailing to create designs that feel both sophisticated and distinctive.

Each creation is approached as a piece of wearable art — designed not only to adorn, but to evoke emotion, presence, and quiet opulence. My design philosophy centres around timeless beauty, elevated craftsmanship, and the belief that true luxury lies in the details.
